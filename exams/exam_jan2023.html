Question 1

Consider the market for cheese where P is the price and Q is the quantity of cheese in kilograms. The market demand is given by \(Q = 6 - 0.5P\). The market supply is given by \(Q = P\). The government mandates a price floor for cheese of 6 CHF per unit. Holding everything else constant, which of the following statements is true?
✅ Correct Answer: A
Equilibrium: 6 - 0.5P = P → 6 = 1.5P → P = 4, Q = 4. Price floor at 6: Qd = 6 - 3 = 3, Qs = 6. Excess supply = 6 - 3 = 3 kg.

Question 2

A baker is a monopolist in a valley. The demand for bread is given by \(Q(P) = 12 - 2P\). The marginal cost is given by the function \(MC(Q) = Q\). Which of the following statements regarding the introduction of a price ceiling is correct?
✅ Correct Answer: A
Demand: Q = 12 - 2P → P = 6 - 0.5Q. MR = 6 - Q. Monopolist: MR = MC → 6 - Q = Q → Q = 3, P = 4.5. Social optimum (P = MC): 6 - 0.5Q = Q → 6 = 1.5Q → Q = 4, P = 4. A price ceiling of 4 maximizes total surplus.

Question 3

Suppose the initial market equilibrium price of the good is $10. Suppose that a unit tax of $2 is imposed on producers. The market has a vertical demand curve and an upward sloping supply curve. Which of the following statements is correct?
✅ Correct Answer: A
With a vertical demand curve, consumers bear the entire burden of the tax. The price paid by consumers increases by the full amount of the tax: 10 + 2 = 12.

Question 5

Yang is living in rural China, and she is about to finish high school. She has to decide whether to attend university and go on studying. If she does, she is expected to pay a tuition fee of around 3,000 CHF in total over the next four years. Alternatively, she can work in a local factory, where she is expected to earn around 20,000 CHF over these 4 years. Which one of the following statements is correct for Yang?
✅ Correct Answer: C
Opportunity cost of university = tuition (3000) + foregone earnings (20,000) = 23,000. If lifetime earnings increase by 24,000, the net benefit is positive (24,000 - 23,000 = 1,000), so she will choose to go.

Question 6

Coop and Migros de-facto form a duopoly in the Swiss grocery retail market. As a direct consequence of the surge in oil prices in the third and fourth quarters of 2022, both firms are considering a change in their pricing policies for cooking oil. The payoff matrix below reports the annual profits of these firms for any combination of the pricing strategies (in million CHF). Which of the following statements is correct? (Matrix: Coop High/Migros High: 6,7; Coop High/Migros Low: 4,9; Coop Low/Migros High: 8,5; Coop Low/Migros Low: 5,6)
✅ Correct Answer: C
Nash equilibrium: If Coop chooses High, Migros' best response is Low (9 > 7). If Coop chooses Low, Migros' best response is Low (6 > 5). So (Low, Low) is a Nash equilibrium. Pareto efficient means no other outcome makes both better off. (High, High) gives (6,7), which is better for Migros but worse for Coop compared to (Low, Low). (Low, High) gives (8,5), which is better for Coop but worse for Migros. So (Low, Low) is not Pareto efficient.

Question 8

Consider a market, where the demand function is given by \(Q^d = 400 - 4P\), and the supply function is given by \(Q^s = 2P - 140\). At the equilibrium, which of the following statements is correct about consumer surplus and producer surplus?
✅ Correct Answer: C
Equilibrium: 400 - 4P = 2P - 140 → 540 = 6P → P = 90, Q = 40. Demand intercept: 400 - 4P = 0 → P = 100. CS = 0.5 * 40 * (100 - 90) = 200. Supply intercept: 2P - 140 = 0 → P = 70. PS = 0.5 * 40 * (90 - 70) = 400.

Question 10

Suppose that the market for cigarettes is in a situation of perfect competition with a demand having the following form: \(Q^D (P) = 10 - P\) and an offer in the following form: \(Q^O (P) = P\). The State decides to introduce a tax of 2 CHF, considering that cigarettes are not expensive enough. What is the value of the deadweight loss (loss of total surplus) associated with the introduction of this tax?
✅ Correct Answer: A
Initial equilibrium: 10 - P = P → P = 5, Q = 5. With tax: Supply becomes Qs = P - 2. New equilibrium: 10 - P = P - 2 → 12 = 2P → P = 6 (consumer). Q = 4. DWL = 0.5 * 2 * (5 - 4) = 1.

Question 12

In a perfectly competitive market, the marginal cost of a firm is given by the following expression: \(MC = 12 + 4q\). If 60 firms occupy the market with this same marginal cost function, what will be the quantity supplied if the price is 20?
✅ Correct Answer: D
Set P = MC: 20 = 12 + 4q → 8 = 4q → q = 2. Each firm supplies 2 units. Market supply = 60 * 2 = 120.

Question 17

Question 18

The graph below shows the profits and operating profits of Air France-KLM from 2005-2015. We see that profits were negative in 2009, 2010 and from 2011-2014, before becoming positive in 2015. Why did the aviation company not shut down during the interim period?
✅ Correct Answer: B
A firm continues to operate in the short run as long as P > AVC (it covers its variable costs), even if P < ATC (it makes a loss). It exits only if P < AVC. Air France-KLM was covering its variable costs but not its total costs.

Question 20

Consider a market where demand is represented by the function: \(Q^{d}(P) = 16 - P\). The marginal cost function of a monopoly firm is given by: \(MC(Q) = Q + 4\). The State assesses the possibility of intervening in the market to force the company to apply marginal cost pricing (in which case the price is equal to the MC of the firm). If the measure is applied, by how much would consumer surplus change?
✅ Correct Answer: C
Monopoly: Demand: P = 16 - Q. MR = 16 - 2Q. MR = MC → 16 - 2Q = Q + 4 → 12 = 3Q → Q = 4, P = 12. CS_monopoly = 0.5 * 4 * (16 - 12) = 8. Social optimum: P = MC → 16 - Q = Q + 4 → 12 = 2Q → Q = 6, P = 10. CS_social = 0.5 * 6 * (16 - 10) = 18. Change = +10.

Question 21

The federal railways (CFF, by its acronym in French), due to the importance of the fixed cost associated with the rail network, have a typical cost structure of natural monopoly, with a constant marginal cost always lower than the average cost (which is always decreasing). The Confederation (the Swiss government) wishes to regulate this natural monopoly. Which of the following statements is correct?
✅ Correct Answer: D
For a natural monopoly, MC < AC. Setting P = MC maximizes consumer surplus and total surplus, but the firm makes a loss because P < AC. Setting P = AC allows the firm to break even but does not maximize surplus.
