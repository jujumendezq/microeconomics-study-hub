Question 1

In September 2022, the director of Payot, the largest bookshop in French-speaking Switzerland, filed a complaint to the Competition Commission (COMCO) against the Madrigall group, which distributes most French books in Switzerland. ✅ Correct Answer: B

Question 2

Suppose that each firm in a perfectly competitive market has the total cost function: \(C(q) = 50 + q^2\). Which of the following expressions gives the aggregate supply curve, given that there are 200 identical firms?
✅ Correct Answer: A
MC = 2q. In perfect competition, P = MC → P = 2q → q = P/2. For 200 firms, Qs = 200 * (P/2) = 100P.

Question 6

All other things being equal, which of the following statements is correct?
✅ Correct Answer: C

Supply: P = Q. With subsidy, supply shifts down: P = Q - 1. New equilibrium: 8 - Q = Q - 1 → 9 = 2Q → Q = 4.5. Price paid by consumers: P = 8 - 4.5 = 3.5. Price received by producers: P = Q - 1 = 3.5. The subsidy per unit is 1. Total cost = 4.5 * 1 = 4.5.

Question 7

A baker is a monopolist in a valley. The demand for bread is given by \(Q_{D} = 12 - P\), where \(P\) is the price of bread. The marginal cost of production is given by \(MC = Q\). Which of the following statements regarding the introduction of a price ceiling is correct?
✅ Correct Answer: B
Demand: P = 12 - Q. MR = 12 - 2Q. Monopoly: MR = MC → 12 - 2Q = Q → Q = 4, P = 8. Social optimum: P = MC → 12 - Q = Q → Q = 6, P = 6. A price ceiling of 6 maximizes total surplus.

Question 12

Suppose that the average cost of producing a pound of cereal is $2, irrespective of the level of output. (This means there are no fixed costs.) Which of the following statements is then correct?
✅ Correct Answer: C
If AC is constant at $2, then TC = 2Q. MC = dTC/dQ = 2. So MC = AC = 2. The two curves coincide.

Question 18

Question 21

In the context of developing countries, how school infrastructure influences the development of human capital continues to be an important question. Drawing from Chinese data from 1999 to 2016, and using standard econometric techniques, economists estimated the following equation relating the enrolment rate in secondary schools (Y) to the number of secondary schools (X): \(Y = 0.07X + 386\). What is the elasticity between secondary school enrolment and the number of secondary schools in China when the number of secondary schools is \(X = 200\)?
✅ Correct Answer: A
Elasticity = (dY/dX) * (X/Y). dY/dX = 0.07. At X=200, Y = 0.07*200 + 386 = 14 + 386 = 400. Elasticity = 0.07 * (200/400) = 0.07 * 0.5 = 0.035.

Question 22

Question 26
