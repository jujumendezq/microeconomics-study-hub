Question 1

Each of the following four choices offers two facts. Which pair of facts is most likely to reflect a causal relationship between the two?
✅ Correct Answer: A
The slump in Ukraine's grain exports caused by the Russian invasion is a clear causal factor for food shortages in Africa and the Middle East. The other options show correlations without clear causality.
B: COVID-19 and temperature records are unrelated.
C: Liz Truss's appointment and the Queen's death are coincidental.
D: Monkeypox outbreak and its endemic regions are correlated but not causally linked in the way described.

Question 2

Car prices rose dramatically in 2022 as a result of global supply chain issues, with a persistent chip shortage holding up production in the auto industry. What do you expect to happen in the U.S. car insurance market?
✅ Correct Answer: B
Car insurance is a complement to cars. When car prices rise due to supply chain issues and chip shortages, fewer cars are bought, leading to a fall in demand for car insurance.
Key concept: Complements have a negative cross-price relationship.

Question 3

The Federal Office for Agriculture announced an increase in butter import quotas. Current milk production was sufficient to meet domestic demand for butter with locally-sourced butter. However, the processing of milk into butter is not economically attractive. They blame cheese exports for being the cause. Which of the following information is correct?
✅ Correct Answer: C
The processing of milk into butter is not economically attractive because cheese exports are more profitable. This means the opportunity cost of producing butter is too high—farmers would rather use milk to produce cheese for export. Therefore, Switzerland needs to import butter.
Key concept: Opportunity cost = value of the best alternative forgone (exporting cheese).

Question 4

On September 25, 2022, the Swiss population accepted the strengthening of the financing of the AVS by means of an increase in the Value Added Tax (VAT) from 7.7% to 8.1%. The burden of this tax increase will fall most heavily:
✅ Correct Answer: D
None of the above is correct because the tax burden is determined by the relative elasticities of demand and supply, not by income elasticity.
The rule: The more inelastic side bears more of the tax burden. A VAT tax increase affects all goods, and the incidence depends on the specific market elasticities.
A is wrong: If demand is elastic and supply is inelastic, producers bear more burden.
B is wrong: If demand is inelastic and supply is elastic, consumers bear more burden.
C is wrong: Income elasticity is not relevant for tax incidence.

Question 5

Consider the market for coffee in Brazil. Suppose some innovation in production practices makes it possible to produce greater amounts of coffee at a lower cost. Everything else remaining constant, what would this innovation lead to?
✅ Correct Answer: B
An innovation that reduces production costs increases supply at every price, causing the supply curve to shift RIGHT.
Result: Lower equilibrium price and higher equilibrium quantity.
Key concept: Changes in technology shift the supply curve (not the demand curve).

Question 6

The graph below reports the average monthly electricity wholesale price in Switzerland from January 2019 to September 2022. TPG and SBB (public transport providers) did not modify their fares during this period. Which implication can you deduct from an inspection of this graph?
✅ Correct Answer: C
Electricity prices rose sharply between July 2021 and July 2022. Since public transport fares remained unchanged, the relative price of private electric commutes increased. This led to a decrease in the quantity demanded of private electric commutes relative to publicly provided commutes.
Key concept: As the relative price of a substitute (private electric commutes) increases, demand shifts toward the alternative (public transport).

Question 7

The graph represents the labor market participation of women and men with children under 6. What can we infer from the parents' decision to work?
✅ Correct Answer: B
The graph shows that the labor force participation gap between women with and without children is smaller in higher-income countries. This means the opportunity cost of child rearing for female parents decreases as the country's average income level increases.
Why? In higher-income countries, there are better childcare options, more flexible work arrangements, and greater support for working mothers.
